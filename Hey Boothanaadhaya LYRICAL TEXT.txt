*LYRICS IN ENGLISH*

*Pallavi*

Hey Bhoothanaadhaaya nee divya paadaalu
Kadilaayi bhuvipaiki idigo pranaamam

Nee maala dharanaalu niyamaala saaraalu
Nee vela naamaalu ne chetu gaanam

Sharanamayyappa ani ante aabharanam
Adi chusi murisindi neelaambaram

Ayyappa smaranam adi paapa haranam
Velugulatho virajallu vishwambharam

Nee raaka ee loka kalyaaname kaada nee mantra naamaalu pranavaaluraa
Nee chetilo velugu deepaalu maa karma paapaalannintini tolaginchuraa..

Swaagatam Ayyappa neeku
Swaamiye venchesaraa
Swaamiye Ayyappa sharanam
Aa maate panchaamrutam

*Charanam-1*

Nalupu vastraalatho modalu deeksha
Medalo rudraakshale manaku raksha
Nadaka saagenu vadili paadaraksha
Onti poota bikshale pareeksha

Channeeti snaanaalu sindoora gandhaalu divvelaa merisenu aa nudutipainaa
Padipooja mantraalu madhura naivedyaalu mangalaa vaadyaalu mogu aapainaa

Swaami deekshante katinamau niyamaavali
Nindu kaartika maasaana deepaavali
Okka mandalam andaram Ayyappalai
Ee bhoomandalam anta dedeepyamai..

Padipooja le chesi panchaamrutam thoni abhisheka haaratulu swaamikiddaam..
Annaprasaadaalu andariki andinchi Ayyappa krupanu manamandukundaam!

*Charanam-2*

Sharamu visiraavanta Shabarimalapai
Sharanamayyappagaa velasi ila pai

Pamba nadi teeraana maatho kalisi
Shabari konda nu ekke varaku nadichi

Paddendi kondalaku guruthugaa velasindi Ayyappa swaami padunettaambadi
Okkokka mettekki thalapaina chuttesi neekappagistaaru aa irumudi

Kanne swaamigaa Ayyappa nee darshanam
Kanula pandage avuthundi maa jeevitam
Maala viraminchi venudirige aa tudi kshanam
Kanulu dhaarale avutaayi swaami sharanam

Jaya jaya dhwaanaalu je je ninaadaalu mogimchi nee munduu mokarillaam
Manasulo anukuntu adugadugu mundesi makara jyothini chusi murisipothaam..!
